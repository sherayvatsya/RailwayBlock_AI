JUDGES & STAKEHOLDERS Q&A CHEATSHEET (Be Prepared For These!) Master Answers for Common Inquiries
Q1: "How does this differ from the existing Indian Railways COA (Control Office Application)?"
Answer: "COA is an execution and train logging tool — it records blocks after controllers have manually agreed on them over the telephone. COA does not perform automated multi-department bundling, spatial clustering, or algorithmic conflict prevention. RailBlock AI sits directly upstream of COA as the intelligent decision-support layer, generating the optimized block plan that COA simply executes."
Q2: "What if an emergency train is delayed by 45 minutes while a block is active?"
Answer: "Our system incorporates a dynamic 15-minute safety buffer. Furthermore, our 'What-If' Simulation Engine receives live GPS feeds from RTIS (Real-Time Train Information System). If a train's arrival projection encroaches on the maintenance window, the system alerts the controller with an automatic rescheduling option before the train reaches the preceding block station."
Q3: "Civil Engineering needs machines, but TRD needs power cutoff. How do you ensure safety when they work together?"
Answer: "Safety interlocking is embedded into our constraint model. A joint Traffic-cum-Power block explicitly sequences the protocols: first, the OHE 25kV power is disconnected and earth discharging rods are placed by TRD staff; only then is the track machine permit released to Civil Engineering. Both clearances must be digitally recorded before the block begins."
Q4: "How computationally scalable is your optimization algorithm across a full railway zone?"
Answer: "Currently, our spatial clustering and greedy heuristic resolve 50 tasks across multiple corridors in under 200 milliseconds in the browser. For zonal scale (1,000+ tasks), our architecture is designed to offload to Google OR-Tools CP-SAT in Python, which solves NP-hard constraint optimization problems within 2 to 5 seconds on standard railway server infrastructure."
